# Course alignment check (verified 27 October 2026)

This project is aligned in topic to the courses below. Nothing is copied from them. Details come from the providers' own pages or from review sites, as noted. Catalogs change, so re-check before you teach from this.

| Course | What was confirmed | What it means for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Google AI Essentials (Grow with Google, Coursera) | Five modules: intro to AI, maximize productivity, prompting, use AI responsibly, stay ahead of the curve. Short course (roughly 5 to 10 hours by review-site reports). Source: summaries and Coursera page, not a full syllabus review. | Weeks 1, 7, 11 map to it. This course is far deeper (about 90 hours). |
| Google Prompting Essentials | Teaches the Task, Context, References, Evaluate, Iterate framework (TCREI). Source: Coursera lecture titles and reviews. | Week 3 teaches TCREI next to this course's own TCFO framework. TCFO is not Google's. |
| DeepLearning.AI: ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers | Nine lessons (Introduction, Guidelines, Iterative, Summarizing, Inferring, Transforming, Expanding, Chatbot, Conclusion), about 1.5 hours, Python and OpenAI API, released 2023. Source: DeepLearning.AI course page. | Developer-focused. Weeks 3 to 5 reference the Guidelines and Iterative lessons. Some examples may look dated. |
| DeepLearning.AI: Generative AI for Everyone | Three modules (Introduction to Generative AI, Generative AI Projects, Generative AI in Business and Society), no coding. Duration reported from about 3 to 6 hours depending on source. | Good companion for Weeks 1 and 2 and the business and society angle of Week 11. |
| Google Cloud: Introduction to Large Language Models | About 1 hour (some sources say 30 to 45 minutes). Covers what LLMs are, use cases, prompt tuning and Google's gen AI tools. | Companion for Week 2. Partly Google-product specific. |
| Elements of AI (University of Helsinki, MinnaLearn) | Six chapters: What is AI?, AI problem solving, Real world AI, Machine learning, Neural networks, Implications. Free. Estimated at about 25 to 50 hours depending on the source. | It predates generative AI and does not teach prompting. Useful for Week 1 (chapters 1 and 4) and Week 11 (chapter 6) only. |
| promptingguide.ai (DAIR.AI) | Free guide covering zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, prompt chaining, RAG, function calling, agents and adversarial prompting. | Good reference for Weeks 4, 5, 9 and 10. |

## Not verified
- **Udemy courses.** No specific course was checked. Udemy catalogs and syllabi change often. Read the current syllabus and recent reviews before relying on one.
- **DeepLearning.AI courses on structured output, agents and RAG.** Existence and content of specific current courses were not checked. The app now points to the catalog instead of naming a course.
- **Anthropic, OpenAI and Google prompt guides** are referenced generally and were not reread line by line.
- **Time claims** for third-party courses come from varying review sites and the providers' own pages. They differ, so treat them as ranges.
